# Rap Hero

Rap any track. Drop a YouTube or YouTube Music link, Rap Hero finds the synced lyrics, and you rap into your phone's mic. Every word you land on the beat scores.

**Play:** open https://nathangm19.github.io/rap-hero in Chrome on Android, then menu > Install app (or Add to Home screen). After that you can share a song from the YouTube or YouTube Music app straight to Rap Hero.

- **Rap (mic):** synced lyrics roll past; your voice is scored word by word: Fire, Clean, OK, Miss. Streaks raise the multiplier; fill the crowd meter and the crowd goes wild for double points.
- **Tap:** four lanes, for when you can't use the mic.
- **Freestyle:** your mic over the track, recorded.
- **2-player battle:** pass the phone, same track, higher score takes it.
- **No lyrics found** (freestyles, deep cuts): paste the lyrics and tap each line in time once. The sync saves on your phone.
- **House beats:** three original tracks with original lyrics, no link needed.

Earbuds recommended. With Bluetooth earbuds, pick the phone's own mic under Mic and timing so the music stays in full quality.

Lyrics come from [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net) and music plays in the official YouTube player, both at play time. Nothing is stored on a server and no lyrics or audio are in this repo. Songs, syncs, scores and calibration live in your phone's browser.
